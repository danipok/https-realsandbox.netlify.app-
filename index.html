LEARNING OUTCOMES

After completing the course, you will be able to:
  • Develop novel ways to transform challenges into opportunities that foster motivation, learning, and sustainable growth
  • Design your own decision-making framework that reflects your values and helps you steer through priorities with confidence and clarity
  • Co-construct diverse opportunities and partnerships based on shared values, creativity, and potential.

CONTENT

For some, the image of entrepreneurship is one of a high-stakes enterprise, grounded in established business principles and shaped by formal strategic frameworks. But what if we imagined it differently—as a play?

Rooted in the principles of Experiential Pattern Matching and facilitated through the Hopscotch Canvas, this course is designed to spark reflection, imagination, and collaboration.

Together, we’ll create a space for playful synergy, where obstacles turn into opportunities, cooperation sparks momentum, and creative entrepreneurs discover what truly drives them.

01 · The idea

Entrepreneurship
as play.

Conventional entrepreneurship is often presented as a high-stakes enterprise shaped by established principles and formal frameworks. This course asks you to imagine it differently.

The sandbox is a space to stay curious, explore the not-yet-known and discover how your inner potential can become a personally meaningful entrepreneurial path.

The course is rooted in Experiential Pattern Matching and facilitated through the Hopscotch Canvas.
